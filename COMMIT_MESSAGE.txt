Distinguish gated/missing prices from invalid ones in seed_products.py

scrape_products.py now legitimately writes many rows with an empty price
for storefronts that gate pricing behind a login wall (BigCommerce B2B
'research professional' pricing), which is expected -- not a data error.
Previously every priceless row was logged as 'invalid price' and silently
dropped.

- Rows with a blank price are now reported as 'no price yet (needs manual
  pricing)' and written to needs_pricing.csv (--pending-output) for
  follow-up, instead of just being dropped.
- Rows with a present-but-unparseable price value are still flagged
  separately as likely data problems.
- Final summary breaks the skip count down by reason.
- --dry-run prints '(no price)' instead of a blank price column so it's
  clear at a glance which rows will need manual pricing.
