# Storefront home surfaces

Mode: Persuade

Approved direction: Axiom Field Editions

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/field-editions-commerce.webp`

## Direction contract

Three storefronts share a coordinated Axiom wordmark family and glass navigation behavior, but each is composed as a different editorial edition.

- Axiom Dispensary: sunlit botanical culture journal; warm paper, forest ink, ember accents; loose overlapping category plates and an organic serif rhythm.
- Axiom Peptides: specimen monograph; mineral white, deep teal, cool mint; strict asymmetric columns, precise data rules, clean scientific sans/serif contrast.
- Axiom Hash: nocturnal collector catalogue; black umber, parchment, amber; cinematic crops, narrow captions, staggered dark product plates.

The first viewport must be dominated by real catalog photography, contain one clear shop action, and reveal useful category or product imagery at the fold. Glass navigation is functional: it preserves legibility over changing photographs and compresses on scroll. Motion is one orchestrated editorial settling transition with a reduced-motion still state.

Do not literalize generated product labels, purity values, origins, testimonials, or claims from the comp. Use only real catalog data and existing verified copy.
